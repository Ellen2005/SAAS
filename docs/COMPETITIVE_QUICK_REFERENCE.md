**A:** "Training is expensive and time-consuming. A Power BI expert costs $60,000+/year salary, plus months of training for each user. When you ask about 'taux de recouvrement,' SAAS knows you mean contribution collection rates and automatically applies the correct calculations. Generic AI can't provide that institutional context."

### Q: "How do we know SAAS will be maintained and updated?"
**A:** "SAAS is cloud-native with automatic updates, unlike on-premise BI tools requiring manual maintenance. Our business model depends on customer success - we succeed when CNPS succeeds. Traditional BI vendors profit from licensing and consulting fees regardless of your outcomes. We're aligned with your success."
