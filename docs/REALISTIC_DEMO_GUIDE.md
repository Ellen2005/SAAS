## 🚨 **Key Anomalies & Issues in the Dataset**

### **1. Contribution Collection Problems**
- **7,254 overdue payments** (15% of total)
- **549+ million FCFA in late fees** accumulated
- **5,481 invalid contribution amounts** (negative, zero, or suspicious)
- **2,945 missing employee SSNs** (data quality issues)

### **2. Regional Performance Disparities**
- **Yaoundé (YAO): 5.2% overdue rate** - highest in country
- **Bafoussam (BAF): 4.7% overdue rate** - best performing
- **Clear performance gaps** between urban and rural offices

### **3. Pension Processing Bottlenecks**
- **261 rejected pension claims** (15% rejection rate)
- **396 claims taking >30 days** to process (20% delays)
- **Average 10.9 days processing** time (target: 7 days)

### **4. Workplace Accident Claim Bias**
- **Bertoua (BER): 53.6% rejection rate** - suspicious bias
- **Kribi (KRI): 47.7% rejection rate** - also problematic
- **Urban offices: ~19% rejection rate** - much better

### **5. Employer Compliance Issues**
- **155 delinquent employers** (10% of total)
- **24 employers with missing company names**
- **9 employers with invalid employee counts** (negative/zero)
